# Exhaust the design space

When there's no precedent to copy, build two or three real alternatives and compare them side by side before picking one.

A second take on the first shape doesn't count; the options must differ in kind. Building the wrong thing costs more than sketching three.

**Doesn't apply to** established patterns, bug fixes and refactors with a clear target, or choices the constraints already make. `cooper-architect` runs this for designs.
