# Human decisions

An existing decision stays binding until Dan changes it. Keep his words and their source on the closed `decision` issue and in every brief it affects. A worker's recommendation is not a decision.

Bring Dan choices about stored data shape, service communication, external providers or fallbacks, money values and limits, other teams' infrastructure or credentials, shared contracts, `human` merges, and anything he already decided. A reversible branch does not transfer those decisions to Dispatch or Cooper.

For a complex item with several open choices, settle them with Dan before the implementation brief. Map dependencies between the questions, gather facts through Cooper, then ask one bounded round at a time. Show the options, their effects and costs, and a recommendation. Use the host's question tool when available. Do not keep asking for permission already granted, and do not start building an unapproved alternative while waiting.

Each open question is a `decision` issue labelled `human` that blocks the work waiting on it, per [project state](project-state.md#decisions). Record the answer on that issue with Dan's exact words, source and time, close it, and link it `related` to every affected item. A Wayfinder ticket's resolution is the same issue. Pause only dependent work at an unresolved gate; other authorized work can continue.
