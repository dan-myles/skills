# Human decisions

An existing decision stays binding until Dan changes it. Keep his words and their source in the brief and decision log. A worker's recommendation is not a decision.

Bring Dan choices about stored data shape, service communication, external providers or fallbacks, money values and limits, other teams' infrastructure or credentials, shared contracts, and anything he already decided. A reversible branch does not transfer those decisions to Dispatch or Cooper.

For a complex item with several open choices, settle them with Dan before the implementation brief. Map dependencies between the questions, gather facts through Cooper, then ask one bounded round at a time. Show the options, their effects and costs, and a recommendation. Use the host's question tool when available. Do not keep asking for permission already granted, and do not start building an unapproved alternative while waiting.

Record answers under the instance's `decisions.tsv`, or the active Wayfinder ticket's canonical resolution with a pointer in that log. Preserve enough context to paste each binding decision into downstream briefs. Pause only dependent work at an unresolved gate; other authorized work can continue.
